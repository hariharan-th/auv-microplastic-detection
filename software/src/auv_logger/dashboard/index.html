How to read these results

The AS7265X and LDR provide optical measurements. They do not by themselves confirm microplastic particles, polymer type, or concentration. Treat any later classification as a research estimate until validated against laboratory ground truth.

The table is populated from the local SQLite logger database. If dry-run mode is enabled, samples are simulated and marked SIMULATION.
